# Community conduct

Discuss code, evidence and ideas respectfully. Give actionable feedback, acknowledge corrections and keep reports relevant to the project. Participation must be free of harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, threats, doxxing and spam.

These rules cover repository issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces. They apply to maintainers and contributors alike. Do not publish another person's private information or redistribute material without permission.

Report public abusive content using GitHub's reporting controls. For a concern that requires private discussion, request a private contact channel from `@ranzevor` without posting sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and restrict participation according to the conduct and its impact. An affected participant may request reconsideration without repeating abusive content or exposing private evidence.

## 简体中文

围绕代码、证据和观点讨论，提供可执行反馈，接受有效纠正，保持内容与项目相关。禁止骚扰、歧视性辱骂、性骚扰、威胁、泄露他人隐私及垃圾信息。

本准则适用于仓库 Issue、PR 及其他由项目管理的空间，维护者与贡献者同样遵守。不得未经许可公开他人私人信息或重新分发材料。

公开违规内容可通过 GitHub 举报功能反馈；需要私下讨论时，向 `@ranzevor` 请求私密联系渠道，不公开敏感细节。维护者可依据行为及实际影响编辑或移除干扰内容、限制参与。受到处理的参与者可申请重新审视，不重复违规内容或泄露私人证据。
